Hisab Kitab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Hisab Kitab is a simple app for recording your daily expenses, income and money lent or borrowed. This page explains what happens to your data.

What we store

The entries you type (amount, date, type, category, the name of the person for a loan, and your note).

Where it is stored

  • Without sign-in: only in your phone's browser storage. Nothing leaves your device.
  • With Google sign-in: in your own Google Drive, in a folder called “Hisab Kitab” in My Drive. It has one file per date (for example 2026-11-12.json) that you can open, download or delete yourself, and a file named _sync-data.json that the app uses to keep your phones in sync.

We do not run a server or database. Your entries are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Google account data

When you sign in, the app asks Google for your name and email address (to show who is signed in) and one limited Drive permission, drive.file, which covers only the files and folder the app itself creates. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files. Your sign-in stays on your device and is not shared.

Hisab Kitab's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Voice entry (optional)

If you tap the mic button (“Bol kar likho”), your browser's own speech service turns your voice into text. In Chrome this is Google's speech service, and on iPhone it is Apple's. The voice goes from your browser to that service only while the mic is on. It is not sent to us. The text only fills the form; nothing is saved until you tap “Entry jodo”. If you don't use the mic, no voice is recorded.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any entry inside the app.
  • To remove everything from Drive: delete the “Hisab Kitab” folder in My Drive.
  • To remove the app's access: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Hinglish mein

Aapka hisab ya to sirf aapke phone mein rehta hai, ya login karne par aapke apne Google Drive mein: "Hisab Kitab" folder mein har tareekh ki ek file, aur sync ke liye ek _sync-data.json file. Hamare paas koi server nahi hai, aapka data humein ya kisi aur ko nahi milta. App aapki baaki Drive files nahi dekh sakta. Mic button dabane par aapki awaaz browser ki speech service (Chrome mein Google) text mein badalti hai; wo humein nahi milti. Data hatana ho to "Hisab Kitab" folder delete kar dijiye.
