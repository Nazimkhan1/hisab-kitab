Hisab Kitab Terms of Use

Last updated: 12 November 2026

By using Hisab Kitab you agree to these terms. Please read them along with the Privacy Policy.

In short: your data is in your hands.
  • Your records live only on your phone, or in your own Google Drive if you sign in.
  • We have no server and no database. We cannot see, read, sell or share your records.
  • You can delete your data or remove the app's access at any time.

1. What the app is

Hisab Kitab is a free tool for keeping your own daily expenses, income and money lent or borrowed. It is a personal record book. It is not a bank, payment service, lending service or financial advice.

2. How your data is kept safe

  • The app maker has no access. The person who made the app has no admin panel, no user list and no way to open, change or delete anyone's records. Everything runs on your phone and goes straight to your own Drive.
  • No server of ours. The app is a web page that runs on your phone. There is no Hisab Kitab server or database that holds anyone's entries, so there is nothing of yours for us to leak.
  • Your own Google Drive. If you sign in with Google, your entries are saved in your Google Drive: a “Hisab Kitab” folder with one file per date that you can open yourself, plus a _sync-data.json file used for syncing. They are protected by your Google account, including any 2-step verification you have turned on.
  • Limited permission. The app asks for one Drive permission, drive.file. It lets the app use only the files and folder it creates itself. It cannot see, open or change your photos, documents or any other Drive files.
  • Sign-in by Google. You sign in on Google's own screen. The app never sees your Google password.
  • Short-lived access. The access Google gives the app expires after about an hour and is kept only in memory. It is not saved on the device or sent anywhere else.
  • Encrypted connection. The app is served over HTTPS, and Google Drive uses encrypted connections, so data moving between your phone and Drive is encrypted on the way.
  • No ads, no tracking. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking code.
  • Open code. The full source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check what it does.

3. What is in your control

  • Use the app without signing in. Entries then stay only on that phone.
  • Sign in or sign out whenever you want.
  • Delete any entry inside the app.
  • Download a backup file with “Backup lo” and restore it with “Backup se wapas lao”.
  • Open, download or delete your daily files in the “Hisab Kitab” folder in My Drive.
  • Delete everything the app saved in Drive: delete the “Hisab Kitab” folder.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Keep your phone locked and your Google account secure. Anyone who can open your phone or your Google account can see your records.
  • If you use the app without signing in, clearing browser data or losing the phone will lose those entries. Take backups.
  • Enter only your own records, and use the app lawfully.
  • Check your own figures. The app adds up what you type; it cannot know if an entry is wrong.

5. No guarantee

The app is provided free and “as is”. We work to keep it correct and safe, but no software can promise to be free of every bug or always available. We are not responsible for any loss arising from use of the app, from wrong entries, or from problems with your device, browser or Google account. Google's own services are covered by Google's terms.

6. Changes

The app and these terms may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated terms.

7. Contact

For questions or problems, open an issue on the GitHub page.
